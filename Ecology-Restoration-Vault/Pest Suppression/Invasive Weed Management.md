# 🌾 Invasive Weed Management

> [!abstract] In one line
> Run **Integrated Weed Management** matched to the **invasion curve** (prevent → eradicate → contain → asset-protect), sequence work **top-of-catchment downstream and least-to-most infested**, use **only aquatic-registered herbicides near water**, and **always restore competitive native cover** — because revegetation without follow-up fails.

Pairs with [[Vertebrate Pest Control]]; near-water herbicide rules in [[Regulation, Incentives & Markets]]; weed-extent monitoring in [[Monitoring Schedules & Triggers]].

> [!danger] Herbicide near water is regulated & time-limited
> Only **aquatic-registered** formulations/surfactants may be used over/beside water, often under **time-limited APVMA minor-use permits** (e.g. metsulfuron-methyl **PER92971**, exp 31/03/2028; glyphosate-360 splatter-gun **PER9907**, exp 31/03/2030). **Never use non-aquatic surfactants near water.** NSW also requires compliance with the *Pesticides Act* + notification. **Confirm current permit & label before any application.** *Sources: [APVMA permits](https://permits.apvma.gov.au/PER82307.PDF); [NSW EPA pesticide use](https://www.epa.nsw.gov.au/Your-environment/Pesticides/pesticide-use-nsw).*

## 🌳 Willows (Salix spp. — Weeds of National Significance)

- **WoNS status:** all willows are WoNS **except** weeping willow *S. babylonica*, pussy willow *S. caprea*, and *S. × reichardtii* (explicitly excluded). *Source: [NSW WeedWise — Willows](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Willows).*
- **Method by size:** **foliar spray** (small/seedlings); **cut-stump** or **stem injection (drill-and-fill / frilling)** for large trees where foliar is impractical. Combining methods beats any single technique. *Source: [NSW WeedWise](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Willows); [Rivers of Carbon](https://riversofcarbon.org.au/resources/willows-willow-management/).*
- **Near water:** **Glyphosate 360** (aquatic-approved) is the herbicide registered for use along waterways for willows. *[verify current label/permit]* *Source: [NSW WeedWise](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Willows).*
- **Stage removal to avoid erosion:** wholesale removal can **destabilise banks** — remove in stages, manage the standing root mass, and establish replacement natives to hold the bank and avoid channel incision/debris dams. *Source: [Willow Weed Management Guide](https://www.sgln.net.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/salix.pdf); [Willows National Management Guide §3](https://connectingcountry.s3.ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/press/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/30153451/Willows_Management_Guide_Sect3.pdf).*
- **Timing:** willows flower in spring (2–3 weeks), seed ripens ~3–4 weeks later — **plan control to prevent seed set**. *Source: [Willows National Management Guide](https://www.sgln.net.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/salix.pdf).*
- **Revegetate after removal** with local natives — an **integral part** of the willow program, not optional. *Source: [Willows National Management Guide §3](https://connectingcountry.s3.ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/press/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/30153451/Willows_Management_Guide_Sect3.pdf).*

## 🌿 Woody weeds & WoNS in MDB riparian zones

- **Riparian WoNS suite:** African boxthorn, blackberry, gorse, boneseed, bridal creeper, plus willows — high-impact, require coordinated programs. *Source: [EPA SA Water Wise No.4](https://www.epa.sa.gov.au/files/8474_waterwise4.pdf); [GWLAP](http://gwlap.org.au/?event=control-prickly-wons).*
- **Blackberry:** **foliar herbicide is the most reliable** route to local eradication; timing (late-summer/autumn before dormancy) is critical. *Source: [Blackberry Best Practice Management Guide (NSW DPI)](https://archive.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0012/347988/blackberry-best-practice-management-guide.pdf).*
- **Blackberry biocontrol** (leaf-rust *Phragmidium violaceum*) **suppresses, won't eradicate**; susceptibility varies by taxon; summer slashing can boost rust effect. *Source: [Victorian Blackberry Taskforce](https://vicblackberrytaskforce.com.au/managing-blackberry/).*
- **Gorse:** the **National Best Practice Manual 2nd ed. (2021)** is the current definitive guide (mapping → planning → control → follow-up). *Source: [Gorse Manual 2021](https://vicgorsetaskforce.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/2-Gorse-National-Best-Practice-Manual.pdf).*
- **Tamarisk/athel pine:** integrated **mechanical + chemical** (hand-pull seedlings, rip/bulldoze large trees), care near water; national manual has a "Decision Support Tree"; **replace with natives** for windbreaks/shade/erosion. *Source: [Athel Pine National Best Practice Manual](https://nre.tas.gov.au/Documents/AthelPine_CRC_bpmg.pdf).*

## 💧 Floodplain forbs/grasses & aquatic weeds

- **Lippia** (*Phyla canescens*) — affects ~**5.3 million ha** of MDB floodplain grazing country; favoured by **river regulation** (reduced flood frequency/duration); **prolonged summer flooding** can switch dominance back to natives. Increases erosion, reduces bank stability. *Source: [NSW DPI Lippia PrimeFact](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0018/342450/Lippia.pdf); [WeedWise](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Lippia).*
- **Alligator weed** (*Alternanthera philoxeroides*) — spreads from stem/root fragments, so **mechanical disturbance is discouraged**; **notifiable/eradication-target** weed — report it; terrestrial vs aquatic infestations need different control. *Source: [NSW WeedWise](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Alligatorweed); [Weeds Australia](https://weeds.org.au/profiles/alligator-weed/).*
  - **Biocontrol** (flea beetle *Agasicles hygrophila*, moth *Arcola malloi*) suppresses **floating aquatic mats** only — **ongoing suppression, not eradication**, and **ineffective on land**. *Source: [Weeds Australia](https://weeds.org.au/profiles/alligator-weed/).*
- **Salvinia** — weevil *Cyrtobagous salviniae* is a landmark success (cleared a 400 ha / ~50,000-t mat at Lake Moondarra in ~15 months) but **climate-limited** — effectiveness drops in cooler temperate NSW (optimal 27–35°C). *Source: [CSIROpedia](https://csiropedia.csiro.au/salvinia-biocontrol/); [NSW DPI Salvinia](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0006/81789/Salvinia.pdf).*
- **Sagittaria** (delta arrowhead) in irrigation channels — **imazapyr on dewatered/drawn-down canals in winter**, left dewatered up to ~8 weeks, ponded ~28 days after refill for photolytic degradation; endothall / repeat high-dose glyphosate are alternatives. *[permit-dependent]* *Source: [NSW WeedWise — Sagittaria](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Sagittaria).*
- **Water hyacinth** — WoNS since 2012; integrated **"control modules"** (mechanical harvesting + herbicide + biocontrol weevils). *Source: [NSW DPI Water Hyacinth Control Modules](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/biosecurity/weeds/weed-control/management-guides/water-hyacinth-control-modules).*
- **Cumbungi** (*Typha*) — combine physical + chemical; **maintain native fringing vegetation to keep light low** and prevent seed germination; *Juncus* helps displace seedlings. *Source: [Weeds Australia — Cumbungi](https://weeds.org.au/profiles/cumbungis-cumbungi-bulrush/).*

## 🎯 Integrated Weed Management (IWM)

- **Four strategies on the invasion curve:** **Prevention → Eradication → Containment → Asset Protection** — early incursions warrant **EDRR/eradication**; widespread weeds shift to **containment/asset protection**. *Source: [Weeds Australia — IWM](https://weeds.org.au/manage/integrated-weed-management/); [Australian Weeds Strategy 2017–2027](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/sites/default/files/sitecollectiondocuments/pests-diseases-weeds/consultation/aws-final.pdf).*
- **Asset-based prioritisation** for widespread weeds: identify high-value biodiversity **assets**, the weeds threatening them, and where control yields greatest benefit/cost — **not blanket control**. *Source: [CSIRO AdaptNRM](https://adaptnrm.csiro.au/invasive-plants-climate-change/weed-management-planning/weed-management-options/).*
- **Catchment sequencing:** work **top-of-catchment downstream** and **least- to most-infested** reaches to prevent re-invasion from upstream. *Source: [CRC riparian weed management guide (Ede & Hunt 2008)](https://archive.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/319448/riparian-habitat-management-guide.pdf).*

```
  Invasion curve →   Prevention   Eradication   Containment   Asset protection
  (cost ↑, feasibility ↓)  ◄── small/new ───────────────── widespread/established ──►
```

## ✅ Follow-up, revegetation & monitoring

- **Follow-up is non-negotiable** — locate and kill regrowth/seedlings over **multiple seasons**; continue weed control + stock exclusion until **replacement natives are fully established**. *Source: [NSW WeedWise](https://weeds.dpi.nsw.gov.au/Weed/Sagittaria); [Berowra Valley Riparian Guidelines](https://www.friendsberowravalley.org.au/riparian-guidelines/).*
- **Revegetation fails without monitoring:** Australian work (2024) found revegetated riparian areas are **frequently re-dominated by weeds** and lack structural diversity/recruitment; a 2026 study found outcomes are **rarely monitored**. **Restore competitive native cover + map/monitor** (increasingly drone-based) to resist re-invasion. → [[Remote Sensing & Data Standards]] *Source: [Foley-Congdon et al. 2024, Restoration Ecology](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/rec.14098).*

> [!tip] The weed-control loop
> **Map → prioritise (asset-based) → treat (right method/timing) → revegetate → follow-up → re-map.** Skipping follow-up or revegetation is the most common way restoration money is wasted.

## ⚠️ Flags
- **Herbicide rates/surfactants/permits** are legally binding and **time-limited** — always confirm current APVMA permit + label; **aquatic-registered only** near water.
- **Foundational manuals** (Willows, Athel Pine, Blackberry, riparian guide) largely date to **2006–2012** — still the cited national best practice but not "new" (Gorse 2021 and biocontrol practitioner manual 2023 are current).
- **Biocontrol is climate-dependent** — salvinia & alligator-weed agents **underperform in cooler temperate MDB sites**; don't assume tropical results transfer south.
